# Fixture privacy policy

Only synthetic or irreversibly redacted fixtures may be committed.

Fixtures must not contain cookies, authorization headers, installation tokens, NTU credentials,
signed URL query strings, student names or identifiers, grades, submissions, feedback, or
unnecessary course prose. URLs must remove queries and replace identifying path segments. Course
text must be reduced to the minimum synthetic excerpt needed by a test.

Real captures and private acceptance evidence belong under ignored `fixtures/private/` or
`evidence/private/` directories and must not be committed.
